The dashboard uses the shared Era design: the Era colours, type and spacing, a light theme beside the dark one that follows the system setting, and the EraInfra wordmark in a header with a green accent band. Dialogs, menus, tooltips, tabs and toasts now run on ui-sdk instead of Radix and sonner, and deleting or revoking something asks in a dialog instead of the browser's confirm box.
